## 2024-05-24 - Auto-Focus in Keyboard-Driven Experiences
**Learning:** In keyboard-centric applications or games (like using WASD/Arrows to dodge), requiring the user to switch to a mouse or press Tab repeatedly to click a post-game action (like "Try Again") severely breaks flow.
**Action:** Always auto-focus the primary call-to-action button when a dialog or modal (such as a Game Over screen) appears, especially if the primary interaction modality was just keyboard.
