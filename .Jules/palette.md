## 2026-09-26 - Modal Dialog Accessibility and Auto-focus
**Learning:** Custom modal dialogs (like the Game Over screen) often lack native accessibility structures. Applying `role="dialog"` and `aria-labelledby` ensures screen readers announce them properly. Additionally, managing focus manually by auto-focusing the primary action (like 'Try Again') greatly improves keyboard usability by preventing users from having to tab through the document to reach the modal content. Adding `:focus-visible` is crucial so the newly focused element is visually distinct without ruining mouse interactions.
**Action:** When introducing or modifying custom modal screens, always ensure proper ARIA dialog attributes are set, focus is routed to the most logical element inside the modal upon opening, and `:focus-visible` styling is present.
